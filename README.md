# 📧 mail-muncher - Organize your email for smart agents

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Latest-blue.svg)](https://github.com/redwanio660-pixel/mail-muncher/releases)

mail-muncher saves your Gmail messages to your hard drive. It translates emails into simple text files and markdown documents. This process creates a clean library of your inbox history. You can use these files to feed data to artificial intelligence tools or automated programs. The software functions as a bridge between your email and your digital assistant.

## 📥 Getting Started

Follow these instructions to use the software on your Windows computer.

1. Visit the [releases page](https://github.com/redwanio660-pixel/mail-muncher/releases) to find the latest version.
2. Look for the file ending in `.exe` under the Assets section.
3. Click that file to start the download.
4. Save the file to a folder you can find, such as your Downloads folder.
5. Double-click the file to open the program.
6. A window will appear to guide you through the setup.

## ⚙️ How It Works

This tool connects to your Gmail account. It reads your messages and creates a backup on your computer. You choose which emails to save. The software keeps the original email format as an `.eml` file. It also creates a readable markdown version. Markdown makes it easy for computers to process the content.

The software runs as a background service. It acts as a server for your other tools. If you use AI agents, they can access these files through a standard connection. This connection allows your agents to read, sort, and understand your mail without manual copy and paste tasks.

## 💻 System Requirements

Your computer needs the following to run this software:

*   Windows 10 or Windows 11.
*   An active internet connection to sync your Gmail account.
*   Enough disk space for your email archives.
*   Authorization to run programs from unknown sources the first time you open the app.

## 🔑 Permissions

The program requires read-only access to your Gmail. You will provide this permission when you sign in during the first launch. The application does not delete or alter your emails. It only copies information to your local machine. This guarantees your original inbox stays safe.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Configuration

1. **Launch the app.** When the screen opens, select the accounts you want to connect.
2. **Authenticate with Google.** A web browser window will open. Sign in with your Google account. Confirm that you want to grant read-only access to mail-muncher.
3. **Choose a location.** Select a folder on your computer where the program should save your emails. Pick a location with plenty of space.
4. **Set your schedule.** Tell the program how often to check for new messages. You can choose to sync once every hour or once every day.
5. **Start the service.** Click the start button. The app will begin saving your inbox to the chosen folder.
6. **Watch the progress.** A status bar shows you if the work is ongoing or finished.

## 🚀 Using the Data

Once the sync finishes, open your chosen folder. You will see a list of folders organized by date or subject. Each email exists as a separate file. You can open these files with any text editor, such as Notepad. If you use a tool that supports markdown, the emails will display with proper formatting. This makes searching through your old mail simple.

## 🛡️ Privacy and Safety

Your data stays on your computer. The program saves files directly to your hard drive. No external server collects or stores your email content. We value your privacy and keep your communications under your control at all times.

## 📈 Improving Results

To get the best performance, keep the program running in your system tray. This area is near your clock on the taskbar. If you close the program entirely, it cannot check for new mail. Minimizing the window keeps the program active but out of your way.

## 🔄 Updating the Software

Check the download page periodically for new versions. When a new version arrives, download the file and run it. The new version will replace the old one while keeping your settings and saved emails intact.

## 💡 Troubleshooting

*   **App does not start:** Right-click the file and choose Run as Administrator.
*   **Cannot access Gmail:** Ensure your internet connection is active. Check that your Google account allows third-party tools.
*   **No files appear:** Confirm the folder location has write permissions. Check your settings to ensure the sync schedule is active.
*   **Slow performance:** Large mailboxes take time to download. Let the program run in the background until the status bar confirms completion.
